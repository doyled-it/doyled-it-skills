# Building a monster for D&D 5e 2024 (cited, edition-labeled)

Every number below is tagged with its source and edition. Do not mix them silently.

## The gap

The 2024 DMG has no monster-creation framework and no "Monster Statistics by Challenge Rating"
table. Reported by Blog of Holding (the DMG offers little beyond "you can reskin") and Alphastream
(the guidance is sparse, with no new official table), and echoed on the D&D Beyond forums. The 2014
DMG did have that table and a step-by-step workshop. (This rests on reviewers; the 2024 DMG text was
not opened directly during research.) So there is no official 2024 build-by-CR method, and you pick
a labeled source.

## Route 2: Forge of Foes quick monster (5e-generic, Sly Flourish)

Fast ("a monster in under a minute"). Not labeled 2024-specific, so treat as 5e-generic.

- Sample row, CR 3 (Forge of Foes): AC and primary save DC 13, 65 HP, primary attack bonus +5, 23
  damage per round, delivered as two attacks of about 12 each (2d8+3).
- Workflow: pick a CR, take base stats from the quick table, trade armor class against hit points,
  improvise attacks to the target damage per round, add one or two powers, improvise saves at the
  table.

The book is a paid product, so only the sample row is quoted here. Buy it for the full table.

## Route 3: Reverse-engineered 2024 Monster Manual (community, most current)

Community work, not official, but tuned to the 2024 Monster Manual (the revised edition, released
February 2025) that your games actually use.

Blog of Holding, "Monster Manual on a Business Card" (license CC BY 4.0), 2024 Monster Manual
medians:

- Hit points: 15 + 15 per CR. (CR 1 is 30, CR 10 is 165, CR 20 is 315.)
- Damage per round: about 7.5 per CR for CR 1 to 20, rounded up to the nearest 5.
- Dragons and legendary monsters deal about 25 percent more damage.

Alphastream, "How to create a monster for revised D&D 5e 2024", reference points (2024 Monster
Manual analysis):

- CR 6 suggests armor class 16.
- CR 8 averages 135 hit points.
- Save DC = 8 + ability modifier + proficiency bonus.
- Multi-target attacks do half damage.
- A limited-use action does about four times an unlimited action's damage.

The full per-CR table in Alphastream's article is published as an image and was not transcribed
here; open it for the intermediate rows.

## Cross-check: 2014 DMG CR table (official, older target)

This is a cross-check on the Route 3 build, not a route of its own. The 2014 DMG CR table is
official and complete, but calibrated to the 2014 Monster Manual. Blog of Holding finds 2024
monsters run about +1 armor class, similar attack bonus and save DC, and higher hit points than the
2014 guidelines. Use the 2014 table only to sanity-check a Route 3 statblock, expecting those
offsets. Its specific per-CR numbers were not retrieved here.

## Recommended method

1. Reskin first (no math needed).
2. If building, use the reverse-engineered 2024 Monster Manual numbers (Blog of Holding for hit
   points and damage, Alphastream for armor class, save DC, and the build procedure) as the primary
   source.
3. Cross-check the result against the 2014 DMG CR benchmarks (expecting the +1 AC and higher HP
   offsets) and against the XP of the closest official 2024 monster.
4. Label every built number with its source and edition in the statblock.

## Numbers not verified

Complete per-CR tables of armor class, attack bonus, and save DC (either edition), and proficiency
bonus by CR, were not sourced during research and are not quoted from memory. Confirm them from the
cited pages before publishing a boss.
